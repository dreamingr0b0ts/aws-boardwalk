Even the contact form is a demo

Everything else on this page points at something you can open, and the form below is no exception. A submission rides the same stack we sell, and both the Terraform and the Lambda source sit in the public repo next to the rest of the boardwalk. Tell us what you're trying to do and you'll hear back from a person within 24 hours. If you'd rather skip the typing and just talk, you can book a free 30-minute consultation straight onto our calendar.

Route of this message

  1. This page A static file on S3 behind CloudFront. No trackers, no cookies.
  2. POST /api/contact Same-origin through CloudFront, so nothing leaves for a third party.
  3. Lambda: www-contact 97 lines of Node, zero npm dependencies, with a honeypot and a daily rate limit. Read the source.
  4. SES Delivered to info@planetek.org with your address on the Reply-To.
  5. A person Reads it and replies within 24 hours. Usually sooner.
