Common questions

For contracting officers and primes

Do you work as a prime or a subcontractor?

Both. We are sized today for subcontract and teaming roles with established primes, and we pursue small prime awards directly. Our 8(a) application is pending.

Is your infrastructure work GovCloud-ready?

Yes. Everything we build is infrastructure as code in Terraform or CloudFormation, and the same patterns deploy to commercial and GovCloud regions. Nothing depends on console click-paths.

How quickly can you respond to an RFI or sources sought?

Small-business fast. When you email info@planetek.org you are corresponding with the engineer who would do the work, not a capture team.
